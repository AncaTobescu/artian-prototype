VISUAL DESIGN SYSTEM

Typography:
- Inter
- Section headings: approximately 22px semibold
- Body text: approximately 14px regular
- Small labels and metadata: approximately 11–12px regular

Colors:
- Ink: #1A1A1A
- Accent: #2D4A3E
- Surface: #F5F4F2
- Border: #D8D3CC
- Main page background: white

Use the dark green accent selectively for primary actions, active states and important status indicators.

Buttons:
- Primary: dark green #2D4A3E with white text
- Secondary: white background with subtle #D8D3CC border

Overall style:
- refined contemporary art marketplace
- clean and calm
- generous whitespace
- subtle borders
- minimal decoration
- no gradients
- no overly rounded cards
- no bright colors
- accessible contrast
- consistent spacing
- product imagery should remain the main visual focus

Use one consistent desktop layout and header across the complete prototype.
